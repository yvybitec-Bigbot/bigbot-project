BigBot: armor for bot treasuries

BigBot is a small project on PulseChain built around one idea: bots that hold tokens should be able to protect them. The BIGBOT token is the key, the Vault Armor is the shield, and the newer pieces (NFTs, BBG and the portal) add ways to use, burn and lock BIGBOT.

This page is an entry point. It explains each piece in plain words so anyone arriving can get oriented. It is not a full technical spec, and a short presentation will follow later.

Draft for review. Not financial advice. Nothing here is a promise of price, profit or return.

What is BigBot?

BigBot started as a tool for bots and AI agents. Bots often keep their funds in a single hot wallet, so if that wallet's key leaks, everything can be drained at once. BigBot's answer is a Vault Armor: a contract that holds the bot's tokens and only lets them out through a slower, controlled path, using BIGBOT as the signal that "arms" a withdrawal.

Although the project is aimed at bots, the protection idea works for anyone who wants their tokens behind an extra lock.

Always check the contract address, not just the name or ticker. Anyone can create a token with a similar name.

Vault Armor (basic armor) Live

Part 1: the shield for a bot's treasury.

A Vault Armor is a contract that is the wallet. Instead of one key that can do everything, it splits power between roles, so a single stolen key is not enough to empty it.

  • Operator: the bot's everyday (hot) key. It can deposit, and it can withdraw only after a withdrawal has been armed.
  • Key master: a separate key that "arms" a withdrawal by paying a BIGBOT signal.
  • Delay and caps: after arming there can be a waiting time, and each arming allows only a limited share of the funds.
  • Fixed exit: withdrawals only go to an exit address chosen when the vault is created.
  • Decoy trap: "emergency" style functions that an attacker might try send the funds to a safe trap address instead.

The version live on mainnet (v2) burns BIGBOT: creating a vault through the factory burns 50 BIGBOT, and each vault can be set to burn BIGBOT every time a withdrawal is armed. The factory has no owner and no admin functions.

Honest limit: a Vault Armor cannot protect funds if one person controls both the operator key and the key master key at the same time.

The 369 BigBot dispenser

A simple contract that sells BIGBOT for PLS at a set price, with no order book and no pool.

Dispenser v1 Live

  • Price: 1,000 PLS per BIGBOT.
  • Maximum per address: 369 BIGBOT.
  • Maximum per day, for everyone together: 20,000 BIGBOT.
  • The price can only go up, never down.
  • Stocked with 369,000 BIGBOT on 27 Sep 2026.

Dispenser v2 Testnet

The new version links buying to the NFTs.

  1. You buy at least 369 BIGBOT. Smaller purchases are rejected.
  2. Every full 369 BIGBOT you buy gives you one claim right (for example, 738 gives two).
  3. You use a claim right to claim a 369 NFT, paying only the gas yourself (see NFTs).
Testnet settings are not the final product settings. On testnet the price is 1 PLS per BIGBOT and the per-address limit is 3,690, only so the tests fit a test budget. The product price stays at 1,000 PLS per BIGBOT. Final mainnet limits will be confirmed before launch.

The reserve and refill system Testnet

Where the BIGBOT for the NFTs comes from, and how it flows back.

Dispenser v2 keeps two separate pots of BIGBOT inside the same contract:

Sale stock

BIGBOT that buyers can purchase with PLS.

Claim reserve

BIGBOT set aside only for NFT claims. Buyers can't buy it, and the owner can't withdraw it.

How it gets refilled

  1. The owner adds BIGBOT to the dispenser and marks it as claim reserve.
  2. Every time someone releases a BBG armor vault, they pay 369 BIGBOT, and those tokens go back to the dispenser instead of being burned.
  3. That way, vault releases help feed the next round of buys and claims.
How it works in the current testnet build: the 369 BIGBOT from a vault release arrive in the dispenser as free stock, and the owner then marks them as claim reserve. Whether this should happen automatically is still a design detail.

NFTs: buy, claim and unwrap Testnet

The 369 NFT (testnet name "BigBot Capsule", symbol BBCAP) is an ERC-721 that holds 369 BIGBOT inside it.

The rules

RuleWhat it means
3 per walletA wallet can claim at most 3 NFTs and can hold at most 3 at a time. A 4th claim, or a transfer that would give a wallet a 4th, is rejected.
Unwrap only with 3You can't open one NFT on its own. Unwrapping takes all 3 at once and releases 3 × 369 = 1,107 BIGBOT.
Once per walletEach wallet can unwrap only once, even if it later collects more NFTs.
50% dump capThe total amount that can ever be unwrapped is capped so that, even if everyone unwrapped and sold at once, the BIGBOT/PLS pool price could not fall by more than 50%. With a standard pool, that means at most about 41.4% of the BIGBOT sitting in the pool. The contract enforces this.
You pay the gasClaiming, unwrapping and fusing are done by the user, who pays their own gas.
The real cap will be calculated from the live BIGBOT/PLS pool just before any mainnet launch. The testnet figure came from a made-up test pool and is not a real number.

Fusing: 6 NFTs make 1 BBG

Instead of unwrapping, 6 NFTs can be fused into 1 BBG. The 6 NFTs are burned, and the BIGBOT that was inside them is burned for good rather than released. Because one wallet can hold only 3, a fuse can combine NFTs from two wallets, as long as the person fusing owns them or has been approved for them.

BBG (BigBot Gold) and its armor vault Testnet

BBG is the "luxury armor" key. The ticker is BBG.

  • How you get it: by fusing 6 of the 369 NFTs. Each fuse makes exactly 1 BBG.
  • What it is: an ERC-20 token with 0 decimals, so 1 BBG is one whole key.
  • What it does: it creates an armor vault and acts as that vault's key. Creating the vault does not burn the BBG.
Some PulseChain NFT collections also use the letters BBG. To avoid confusion, the guide and the portal will always show BBG together with its contract address. BBG has no mainnet contract yet. The testnet build still uses the earlier working symbol BBGOLD; the mainnet ticker will be BBG.

How the BBG armor vault works

  1. Create: the BBG holder creates a vault and chooses an exit address. That address is fixed forever.
  2. Locked from the start: the vault begins locked. Anyone can deposit tokens into it.
  3. Only PLS can leave while locked, so the owner can always pay gas. Every other token stays put.
  4. Release: the owner sends 369 BIGBOT. Those go back to the dispenser (see Reserve and refill) and the vault unlocks.
  5. Withdraw: once unlocked, tokens can only go to the fixed exit address, nowhere else.
  6. Relock: the owner can lock the vault again as long as they still hold at least 1 BBG.

BigBot Platinum Live

Deployed on PulseChain mainnet (chain ID 369) on 29 Oct 2026, between 09:41 and 09:43 (London time, GMT), blocks 27,920,349 to 27,920,354. There are 5 contracts, all ownerless and with verified code.

What it is

  • PLATINUM is a standard ERC20 token (OpenZeppelin) with a fixed supply of 21,000 PLATINUM and 8 decimals, minted once at deployment.
  • No owner, no mint and no transfer rules: nobody can create more, and there are no fees or per-transfer limits. It moves freely like any ERC20.
  • Initial distribution: 16,800 (80%) to the dispenser · 3,150 (15%) to the BB swap · 1,050 (5%) to a project timelock.
  • What it's for: every dead man's switch (inheritance) needs a deposit of 1 PLATINUM and 1 BB.

The dispenser: 1 PLATINUM = 1,000,000 PLS

  • Fixed price, no increases. 16,800 PLATINUM are for sale. Fractions are allowed (minimum 0.00000001 PLATINUM = 0.01 PLS).
  • Where your PLS goes: of every payment, 50% is sent to the dead address 0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD in the same transaction, and the other 50% stays locked forever inside the dispenser.
  • The contract has no withdraw function (neither for PLS nor for tokens), no owner and is not upgradeable: nobody, not even the team, can ever take that PLS out.

BB → PLATINUM swap (1 to 1)

You send BB to the BBPlatinumSwap contract and receive the same amount of PLATINUM: 1 BB = 1 PLATINUM, until the 3,150 PLATINUM run out. The BB received go straight to the dead address 0x…dEaD, so every swap reduces the BIGBOT supply. First you approve your BB for the contract, then you call swap().

Project timelock (5%)

The ProjectTimelock contract holds 1,050 PLATINUM (5%) locked for 365 days. From 29 Oct 2027 (09:42:55 UTC) anyone can call release(), but the tokens can only go to the project address 0xB35b673031576d7a95Df5C6F94639A7dD1A0Bfa4. Before that date they cannot be moved.

The dead man's switch (inheritance)

A simple inheritance for your wallet: if you stop giving signs of life for the period you choose, your protected tokens move to your heir's wallet. Your tokens stay in your wallet until then.

  1. Arm: you deposit 1 BB and 1 PLATINUM in the contract, choose the heir wallet (it can be empty), the period (between 90 and 3,650 days) and the tokens to protect, and approve those tokens for the contract. The heir, the period and the list are fixed: they cannot be changed and there is no disarm function. One switch per wallet, forever: that wallet can never arm another one, not even after the release.
  2. Heartbeat: heartbeat() resets the timer. As long as you give a sign of life before the period runs out, nothing happens. Use a gas limit of 50,000 or more (the wallet's automatic estimate can fall short).
  3. Trigger: if the period runs out with no heartbeat, anyone can call trigger(), which records the block. From that moment it cannot be stopped: the heartbeat no longer works.
  4. Release: after 369 blocks (about an hour), anyone calls release() and, in a single transaction: the deposited BB goes to the dead address, the protected tokens still in your wallet (up to the approved amount) move to the heir, and the deposited PLATINUM is sent to the heir.

Only these 10 tokens can be protected

The list is fixed in the contract and cannot be extended. There are two WBTC and two DAI with the same symbol: tell them apart by the address.

Limitations, plainly: native PLS is not protected: wrap it into WPLS first. This is a permission system, not a safe: if you revoke an approve or empty the wallet, there is nothing to move. If your key is stolen, the thief can also send heartbeats. If a token fails to transfer, it is skipped and stays in your wallet; the rest of the release goes ahead. PLATINUM itself cannot be protected, and the heir cannot be your own wallet or the dead address. For release() use the gas estimate: with too little gas it reverts entirely.

Security and review

  • The external code review was done by Grok (AI). It found no critical or high issues; the improvements it suggested were applied before deployment (among them, a per-token gas limit in release()).
  • 67 tests pass on a copy (fork) of PulseChain mainnet, including tests with the 10 real tokens.
  • Verified code: all 5 contracts match on Sourcify (partial match: the bytecode is identical and only the metadata hash differs). On PulseScan, BigBotPlatinum already shows as verified and the other 4 were in the verification queue when this page was published.
  • No contract has an owner (owner() does not exist): no pause, no mint, no withdrawals and no upgrades. The only address with any permission is the timelock beneficiary, and only to receive the 1,050 PLATINUM from 29 Oct 2027.
  • There is no audit by a professional firm. Use it knowing that everything is immutable: a bug after deployment cannot be fixed without deploying new contracts.

Part 3: the GUI (planned) Planned

A web portal that connects to your own wallet and puts Parts 1 and 2 in one place. It will be built and tested against Testnet V4 before anything goes to mainnet.

The portal can never move your funds by itself. It only reads public data from your wallet and the blockchain, and when you want to do something it prepares the transaction and asks you to sign it in your wallet. If you don't sign, nothing happens.

What you will see

  • Your BIGBOT balance
  • Your 369 NFTs (0 to 3)
  • Your BBG, shown with its contract address
  • What's inside each of your vaults
  • Whether each vault is locked or unlocked
  • Each vault's fixed exit address
  • Read-only info about the connected wallet, such as PLS and token balances

What you can do (you sign each one)

  • Buy BIGBOT from the dispenser
  • Claim NFTs
  • Unwrap all 3 NFTs
  • Fuse 6 NFTs into BBG
  • Create a vault with BBG
  • Deposit into a vault
  • Lock and relock a vault
  • Release a vault with 369 BIGBOT

What is fixed (nobody can change it)

  • A vault's exit address, once chosen
  • The rules: 369 minimum, 3 NFTs per wallet, unwrap only with 3 and only once, the 50% cap, 6 NFTs per BBG, 369 BIGBOT to release
  • The portal cannot change these rules; they live in the contracts

Ideas being considered

  • Alerts: a pending NFT claim, 3 NFTs ready to unwrap, progress toward a BBG.
  • A safety check that warns about risky token approvals on your wallet.
  • A history of your purchases, claims and vault activity.
